Health Brain

A personal record of training and everyday health.

One journal, wherever you train

Health Brain records workouts, bodyweight, and personal notes through text or voice. It keeps exercise history and corrections together so previous sets can inform the next workout.

It is a private application operated by Taylor Perry for personal use. There is no public registration. This website describes the application; the journal itself is available only through its owner's private network.

Optional device connections

With the account owner's permission, the Google Health connection imports sleep, exercise sessions, heart rate variability, and resting heart rate from cloud-synced wearable history. These observations appear alongside the journal to provide context over time.

Google access is read-only. Health Brain requests sleep, health metrics and measurements, and activity and fitness permissions. It does not request location or permission to change Google Health records.

A separate, optional Beyond Cortex connection records Voltra exercise telemetry. Workouts entered by hand remain useful without either device connection.

Private by design

The journal and its imported records are stored on the owner's server. Voice transcription and command interpretation run locally. Google Health data is used to display personal health history, not for advertising or training models.

Read the privacy policy for the data collected, retention, and deletion process, and the terms of use for how the application is offered.
